# Golden's Systems Lab

**Golden's Systems Lab (GSL)** is an independent software engineering organization focused on building software systems, reusable frameworks, development tools, and custom solutions.

GSL brings together a diverse range of projects, from game technology and foundational frameworks to internal tooling, experimental software, and solutions developed for clients. Each project is built around a practical purpose, with an emphasis on modularity, configurability, reusability, and maintainable engineering.

Our work spans both public and private development, bringing together independent products, shared technologies, and custom implementations under one engineering vision.

## Areas of Development

* **Frameworks & Infrastructure:** Reusable foundations, shared interfaces, and supporting systems for complex software projects.
* **Game Technology:** Gameplay frameworks, character controllers, combat systems, input handling, and other interactive systems.
* **Animation Technology:** Animation frameworks, rig integration, retargeting, and specialized development tools.
* **Developer Tooling:** Utilities, debugging tools, workflow improvements, and software designed to simplify development.
* **Custom Software:** Tools, systems, and tailored solutions developed for specific projects and client requirements.
* **Research & Experimentation:** Prototypes and experimental technologies exploring architecture, performance, automation, and system integration.

## Projects & Products

GSL maintains and develops a growing portfolio of projects, including:

* **GEngine:** Animation technology and foundational systems for game development.
* **Axon:** A reusable input and control framework.
* **APGS:** A modular gameplay and weapon systems framework.
* **AGE:** An abstract game engine initiative bringing multiple systems together.
* **Animatrix:** Animation-focused development tooling.

These projects represent only part of GSL's work. Additional projects, internal technologies, and client-specific solutions may remain private or be developed independently.

## Engineering Philosophy

GSL values practical engineering over unnecessary complexity.

* **Modularity:** Systems with clear responsibilities and composable components.
* **Reusability:** Technologies designed to serve multiple projects and use cases.
* **Configurability:** Flexible systems that adapt to different requirements.
* **Practical Abstraction:** Architecture that solves real problems without needless layers.
* **Continuous Improvement:** Iterative development, testing, experimentation, and refinement.

The objective is simple: build software that is useful, adaptable, maintainable, and worth building upon.

## Community & Collaboration

GSL is an evolving engineering workspace where ideas become implementations and systems improve through practical use.

Feedback, technical discussions, bug reports, contributions, and professional collaborations are welcome. Public repositories provide an opportunity to explore selected projects and engage with their development.
